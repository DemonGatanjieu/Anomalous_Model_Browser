# 📈 Anomalous Model Browser Changelog

## v1.63.0 — unreleased

- **修一处（比如一只手）更容易成**：从 Codex 这几天修手的试验看，重画的力度太大时，新画的手会撑满你圈的范围，于是手越修越大。现在“局部重绘”默认力度降到修东西用的 0.45（官方节点那块 0.5），AI 也会被告知：圈得贴着手、保持低力度、一次出 4 张，再用一个**只放大手那一块**的挑选面板给你挑，挑完用同样放大的前后对比给你看。展台上的“挑一张”和前后对比滑块都能只显示每张图的同一小块。
- **Fixing one spot (a hand) works more often**: Codex's hand tests showed that with a strong repaint the new hand fills the whole marked area and grows each time. The repaint block's strength is now 0.45 by default, a fix (0.5 for the built-in one), and the AI is told to mark tightly, keep it low, make 4 tries and let you pick in a picks panel **zoomed on the hand**, then show before and after zoomed the same. The stage's picks and slider can show the same small part of every picture.

- **AI 交给“加载图像”的图不再被误报“没选图片”**：AI 拿输出文件夹里的图去重画时，AMB 以前在节点里写“名字 [output]”，ComfyUI 重启、运行记录清空后，新版前端会把它标成“缺少图片”，其实图还在。现在和图库右键“放到画布”一样，先把图复制一份进 input 文件夹（同一张图只存一份，透明部分保留）再填进去。ComfyUI 页面上标红的“缺少图片/视频”，AI 用 get_situation 看现状时也能看到，并知道怎么补。
- **Pictures the AI gives a Load Image are no longer flagged missing**: for an output picture Anomalous used to write "name [output]", which ComfyUI's newer page marks as a missing file once its run history is gone (after a restart), though the file is there. Now it is copied into the input folder first (one copy per picture, transparency kept), as the gallery's "Put on the canvas" does. Files ComfyUI's page marks missing also reach the AI in get_situation, with how to fix them.

- **让 AI 一次出几张时，每张都不一样**：以前种子设成“固定”时，AI 说一次出 4 张，ComfyUI 会把 4 次当成同一个任务，只出 1 张。现在一次出几张时，固定的种子会一张一张往上加（123、124、125…），出完画布上的种子还是原来的数、还是“固定”。修手、修脸这种看运气的活，可以一次出 4 张让你挑。
- **A batch of tries from the AI gives different pictures**: with a seed set to "fixed", asking for 4 used to queue the same prompt 4 times and ComfyUI made 1 picture. Now a fixed seed counts up for the batch (123, 124, 125…) and is as it was afterwards, still fixed. Hands and faces, which depend on luck, can come 4 at a time for you to pick from.

- 跑图前的尺寸提醒多算了一步：放大模型之后**最后要存多大**。要存出超过约 6400 万像素的图（比如 5120×3328 再放大 4 倍，存出来 20480×13312、PNG 约 300 MB），时间线上会出现黄色提醒，AI 也会收到，建议放大倍数小一点或者从小一点的图开始放大。
- The size check before a run now also counts **what is finally saved** after an upscale model: a picture past about 64 megapixels (5120×3328 upscaled 4x is 20480×13312, about 300 MB as PNG) gets a yellow warning on the timeline, and the AI is told, with the advice to upscale less or start from a smaller picture.

- **超大图不再卡住图库**：几亿像素的图（比如把大图再放大 4 倍，存出来几百 MB），以前缩略图做不出来时，AMB 会把整张原图当缩略图发给浏览器，图库和 AI 工作台一翻到它就卡。现在缩略图在后台单独的进程里做一次就存下来（2.7 亿像素的图约 6 秒），不占 ComfyUI 出图的内存；大到连这样也做不了的，卡片上显示尺寸和大小，不再加载原图。卡片角上标“超大图 · 324 MB”。点开看大图、图片详情、AI 工作台里放大看的，都是缩到 4096 像素的预览，下面写着原图多大，要看原图点“打开原图”（在浏览器新标签页里打开）。图片详情读参数也不再把整张图下载下来。
- **Very large pictures no longer freeze the gallery**: for a picture of hundreds of megapixels (a big one upscaled 4x, hundreds of MB), a card image that could not be made used to be the whole original, sent to the browser every time the gallery or the AI workbench showed it. Now it is made once in a separate background process and kept (about 6 s for 272 megapixels), outside ComfyUI's own memory; one too large even for that shows its size instead, never the original. Its card says "Very large · 324 MB". The large view, the image details and the AI workbench's larger pictures show a 4096 px preview with a note saying how large the original is, and "Open the original" opens it in a new browser tab. Reading a picture's settings in its details no longer downloads the whole file.

- 做法按用途合并成 7 个：局部重画、换背景、放大、换模型精修、照着画、修脸修手、图片处理。AI 只说"用途 + 想要哪种"（比如"放大，2x"、"照着画，姿势"），AMB 按你装了什么、画布上是什么模型，自动挑能跑的、效果最好的那一块；没装的照样按节点包列出，AI 能告诉你该装什么。开新任务时 AI 只看到 7 行，做法再多也不会变长。
- The ways are grouped into 7 uses: repaint, new background, upscale, redraw with another model, follow a picture, faces and hands, picture work. The AI names a use and the kind it wants ("upscale, 2x", "follow, pose") and Anomalous picks the best block this ComfyUI can run, by what is installed and the canvas's model; the missing ones are still listed by pack, so the AI can say what to install. A new task's directory is 7 lines, however many ways there are.

- 新做法 **换画风·构图不变**：你递给 AI 一张图，AMB 读出它的轮廓线（ComfyUI 自带的 Canny），用 Union 控制网钉住构图，再按提示词里的新画风整张重画：人物、姿势、位置不变，画法换掉（比如水彩、厚涂、平涂）。实测强度 0.8 只是变亮一点，1.0 才真正换了画风，轮廓线照样守住构图，所以默认 1.0。RTX 4050 6GB、SDXL 832×1216 约 41 秒。
- New way **New style, same layout**: from a picture handed to the AI, its outlines (ComfyUI's own Canny) hold the layout through the union ControlNet while the whole picture is redrawn in the prompt's new style (watercolour, painterly, flat colours): who, pose and place stay. Tried: at denoise 0.8 it only got lighter, at 1.0 the style changed and the outlines still held the layout, so 1.0 it is. About 41 s for SDXL 832×1216 on an RTX 4050 6 GB.

- 新做法 **换模型精修**：出完图以后，换另一个单文件模型（SD1.5、SDXL 各家族之间都能换，配它自己的 LoRA；Anima 这种分三个文件的还不行）把整张图轻轻重画一遍（强度 0.4），另存一张（文件名以 refined 开头）。一个模型的构图加上另一个模型的画风和细节。新模型有自己的提示词节点，先照抄原来的提示词。实测 RTX 4050 6GB、Illustrious 出图 → NoobAI 精修，832×1216 整次约 66 秒。
- New way **Refine with another model**: after a picture is made, another single-file checkpoint (any SD 1.5 or SDXL family, with its own LoRAs; not yet a three-file model like Anima) redraws it lightly (0.4) and saves it beside it (prefix refined): one model's composition with another's style and detail. The new model has prompts of its own, copied from the first. About 66 s for a whole run, Illustrious then NoobAI at 832×1216, on an RTX 4050 6 GB.

- 新做法 **放进另一张图**：你递给 AI 两张图，一张有人物、一张是背景。AMB 抠出人物，把背景缩放裁切成同样大小，贴上人物，再把人物以外的地方轻轻重画一遍（强度 0.5），让脚下的影子、地面和光线接上，背景的样子基本不变。实测 RTX 4050 6GB、SDXL 832×1216 约 40 秒。做法现在可以接第二张图了（`add_nodes` 的 `scene`）。
- New way **Put a character into a picture**: from two pictures handed to the AI, a character and a place, the character is cut out, the place scaled and cropped to its size, the character pasted on, and everything else lightly redrawn (0.5) so shadow, ground and light meet it while the place keeps its look. About 40 s for SDXL 832×1216 on an RTX 4050 6 GB. A block can take a second picture now (`add_nodes`' `scene`).

- 新做法 **换场景·人物不变**：你递给 AI 一张图，AMB 自动抠出人物（inspyrenet-rembg），只重画人物以外的地方，AI 在提示词里写新场景；人物边缘留 6 像素一起重画，接缝更自然。实测 RTX 4050 6GB、SDXL 832×1216 约 30 秒。人物身上的光还是原图的光，换到光线差别很大的场景时看得出来（以后可以加重新打光）。AI 递给做法的图也认 `名字.png [output]` 这种写法了。
- New way **New scene, same character**: from a picture handed to the AI, the character is cut out (inspyrenet-rembg) and only the rest is drawn anew from the prompt's new scene; 6 px of its edge are redrawn too for the seam. About 30 s for SDXL 832×1216 on an RTX 4050 6 GB. The character keeps the old picture's light, which shows in a very differently lit scene (relighting may come later). A picture handed to a block may also be written `name.png [output]`.

- AI 有了现成的做法可以照着用（参考做法）：从擅长这些活的工作流里提炼出来的几块节点，AI 说一声用哪块，AMB 就在画布上搭好、接进你的工作流里，一次调用，不用 AI 一个个节点地搭。AI 开工时会收到这份清单，每块一行。
  - **局部重绘·裁切缝合**：把圈出的地方连同周围一圈一起裁出来，放大到 1024 重画，再缝回原图，别处一点不动。AMB 顺手把采样强度调到 0.45（修东西的力度：再高，新画的东西会撑满你圈的范围，手会变大），并提醒 AI 圈得贴着要修的东西、把提示词改成只写这块地方（比如“一只手，五根手指”），不然它会把整张图的内容（比如一整个人）画进这一小块里；手和脸一次出 4 张让你挑。需要节点包 ComfyUI-Inpaint-CropAndStitch。
  - **局部重绘（官方节点）**：没装上面那个包时用它，只用 ComfyUI 自带的节点。
  - **部位细化（脸和手）**：出完图以后找出脸和手，各自放大重画再贴回去，细化后的图另存一张（文件名以 detailed 开头）。需要 Impact Pack 和 Impact Subpack，以及 models/ultralytics/bbox 里的检测模型。
  - **局部重绘·LanPaint**：把采样器整个换成 LanPaint 的（步数、CFG、种子照搬原来的），它每一步都多想几遍，新画的部分和周围融合得最好，任何模型都能用，不用专门的重绘模型；代价是慢好几倍。需要节点包 LanPaint。
  - **放大**：用放大模型放大到 1.25 倍，再用同样的设置轻轻重画一遍（强度 0.35），细节是真画出来的而不是糊的，另存一张（文件名以 upscaled 开头）。只用 ComfyUI 自带的节点，自动挑你装的放大模型。参考工作流作者也只用 1.25 倍，6GB 显存的卡上 1.5 倍就太吃力了。
  - **分块放大**：放大 2 倍，按 1024 的小块逐块重画，多大的图都放得下，比上一个慢。需要节点包 ComfyUI_UltimateSDUpscale。
  - **照着姿势画**、**照着构图画**：你递给 AI 一张图，AMB 读出它的姿势（OpenPose）或深度（Depth Anything V2），用控制网引导出图。控制网模型由 AMB 按画布上底模的类型自动挑（SDXL 的配 SDXL 的，同底模的优先），参考图自动复制一份到输入文件夹（只新建，不覆盖）。需要节点包 comfyui_controlnet_aux。
  - **迭代放大**：分 3 小步放大到 1.3 倍，每步轻轻重画，比一步到位更稳。用 Impact Pack。
  - **双模型放大**：和"放大"一样，但重画换一个更稳的模型（底模放大时容易走样就用它）；新加的模型加载器先是同一个模型，AI 按你的意思换。只用自带节点。
  - **背景移除**：把一张图的背景抠掉，存成透明 PNG（文件名以 nobg 开头）。需要节点包 comfyui-inspyrenet-rembg。
  - **读图出标签**：你递给 AI 一张图，WD14 打标器读出它的 Danbooru 标签，AI 照着写提示词。需要节点包 ComfyUI-WD14-Tagger，第一次用时会下载打标模型（约 1.2GB）。AI 运行后，节点输出的文字（比如这些标签）现在也会带回给它。
  - 装了 KJNodes 时，几种放大都会在最后把颜色对齐到原图（参考工作流的做法），防止放大后颜色跑偏；没装也照常能用，面板上写着"装了 KJNodes 会更好"。
  - 照着姿势画改用 DWPose 读姿势：二次元人物也认得准，连脸和手指都标（原来的 OpenPose 认二次元常认错）。人物挤成一团、画风很浓的图可能认不出，读出的骨架图或深度图会作为预览给 AI 看，空的它就知道换照着构图画。第一次用时会下载 DWPose 的模型（约 350MB）。
  - **平铺放大**：放大到 1.25 倍，重画时用平铺控制网把画面钉住（强度 0.6，到 80% 的步数，参考作者的参数），所以重画强度可以调到 0.5，细节多得多，构图也不跑。SDXL 用 Union 控制网，NoobAI/Illustrious 优先用 NoobAI 的平铺控制网，Anima 用 Anima 的修复 LLLite（较慢）。
  - **线稿上色**：你递给 AI 一张黑白线稿，AMB 把线条交给线稿控制网（Union 的线稿模式），AI 在提示词里写颜色和画风，按线稿画出彩色图。
  - Union（万能）控制网用之前，AMB 会告诉它这次是姿势、深度、平铺还是线稿。还没扫描过的新控制网按它所在的文件夹（SDXL_1.0、NoobAI……）认底模。做法说缺某个模型时，AMB 先把 ComfyUI 的文件列表刷新一次再找：刚下载到子文件夹里的模型，ComfyUI 自己不重启是看不见的。
  - 放大类的块优先用"够用的最小倍数"的放大模型（放大到 1.25 倍用 2 倍模型，比 4 倍模型再缩回来省事）。
  - 做法会按画布上的底模换搭法：画布上是 Anima 模型时，照着构图画、照着姿势画改用 Anima 专用的 LLLite 控制网，局部重绘·裁切缝合会加上 Anima 的重绘 LLLite。AI 照样只说用哪块。需要节点包 ComfyUI-Anima-LLLite 和 models/controlnet 里的 anima-lllite 模型。Anima 的 LLLite 比 SDXL 的控制网温和，提示词要和参考图说的一致（几个人、在干什么），AI 会被这样提醒。
  - 画布上有好几个采样器时，AI 要指明用哪一个，AMB 不乱猜。
- AI 工作台右边多了**做法**面板：按"要做的事"分组列出所有做法，每块写着能干什么、从哪学来的。缺节点包或缺模型的变灰，写明缺什么，点缺的节点包会打开 ComfyUI-Manager 去搜它。每块有个开关：关掉的，AI 就看不到，硬要用也会被拒绝。AI 开工时只收到开着又能用的做法，其余的只附一行"装了什么还能用什么"。
- 做法面板上每块写着怎么测过的：在哪张卡上（目前都是 RTX 4050 笔记本，6GB 显存）、多大的图、整次运行用了多久；参数也是按这张卡定的（比如放大只到 1.25 倍）。AI 用某块时也会收到这句，能提前告诉你大概要等多久。
- AMB 维护一份做法用到的模型清单（api/block_models.json）：每个模型的下载链接（HuggingFace、Civitai、GitHub）、大小、SHA256、许可证和该放的文件夹。每块做法列出它推荐的模型，面板上已有的打勾（改过名的也认得出），缺的点一下就用 AMB 的下载器下载，下完核对哈希。把做法分享给别人时，对方也能照着这份清单拿到一模一样的文件。
- 线稿上色在 Anima 模型上改用 Anima 的线稿 LLLite。
- 每块做法现在是一个单独的数据文件（api/blocks/），里面有它的节点、要的节点包和模型、给 AI 的话、面板上的中英文说明。以后收集到新做法，加一个文件就行。
- AI has ready-made ways of doing things (reference blocks): a few groups of nodes distilled from workflows that do one job well; the AI names one and Anomalous builds it on the canvas, spliced into your workflow, in one call instead of node by node. The AI gets their list, a line each, when it begins a task.
  - **Repaint, crop and stitch**: crops the marked area with a margin around it, repaints it at 1024 and stitches it back, the rest untouched. Anomalous sets the sampler's denoise to 0.45 (a fix: higher, the new thing fills the whole mark and a hand comes out too big) and tells the AI to mark tightly, write the prompt for that area only ("a hand, five fingers"), or it paints the whole picture's subject (a whole person) into it, and to make 4 of a hand or face for you to pick. Needs ComfyUI-Inpaint-CropAndStitch.
  - **Repaint (built-in nodes)**: for when that pack is not installed, ComfyUI's own nodes only.
  - **Face and hand detailing**: after a picture is made, finds faces and hands, repaints each larger and puts it back, saving the detailed picture beside it (prefix detailed). Needs Impact Pack and Impact Subpack and detector models in models/ultralytics/bbox.
  - **Repaint with LanPaint**: the sampler becomes LanPaint's (same steps, cfg and seed), which thinks several times a step, so the new part blends in best, with any model and no inpainting model; several times slower. Needs LanPaint.
  - **Upscale**: an upscale model enlarges the picture to 1.25×, then a light redraw with the same settings (denoise 0.35) makes the new detail real rather than smeared; saved beside it (prefix upscaled). ComfyUI's own nodes, with an upscale model you have. 1.25× is what the source workflow used; 1.5× is too much for a 6 GB card.
  - **Tiled upscale**: 2×, redrawn in 1024 tiles, so any size fits; slower. Needs ComfyUI_UltimateSDUpscale.
  - **Follow a pose**, **follow a layout**: from a picture you hand the AI, Anomalous reads the pose (OpenPose) or depth (Depth Anything V2) and steers the picture with a ControlNet it picks to fit the canvas's model (SDXL with SDXL, the very same base first); the picture is copied into the input folder, never over a file. Needs comfyui_controlnet_aux.
  - **Iterative upscale**: 1.3× in 3 small steps, a light redraw each, steadier than one jump. Uses Impact Pack.
  - **Upscale with a second model**: like Upscale, but a second, steadier model redraws it (for a base model that goes wrong enlarged); its new loader starts as the same model and the AI changes it as you say. Built-in nodes only.
  - **Remove the background**: saves a picture with its background cut away as a transparent PNG (prefix nobg). Needs comfyui-inspyrenet-rembg.
  - **Tags from a picture**: from a picture you hand the AI, the WD14 tagger reads its Danbooru tags for the AI to write the prompt from. Needs ComfyUI-WD14-Tagger; the first time it downloads its model (about 1.2 GB). What a run's nodes show as words (these tags) now reaches the AI with the run.
  - With KJNodes, the upscales match their colours to the original at the end (as the source workflow does), so they don't drift; without it they work as before and the panel says they're better with KJNodes.
  - Follow a pose reads the pose with DWPose: anime characters too, face and fingers included (OpenPose often misread them). A crowded or very stylised picture may come out empty; the pose skeleton or depth map is left as a preview for the AI, which then turns to Follow a layout. The first time, DWPose downloads its models (about 350 MB).
  - **Upscale held by tile**: 1.25×, a tile ControlNet holding the picture while it is redrawn (strength 0.6 to 80% of the steps, the source's), so the redraw can be stronger (0.5): much more detail, the layout kept. SDXL uses the union ControlNet, NoobAI and Illustrious NoobAI's tile one first, Anima its tile-repair LLLite (slower).
  - **Colour a line drawing**: from a black-on-white drawing you hand the AI, its lines steer a lineart ControlNet (the union one's lineart mode) while the prompt says the colours and style.
  - A union ControlNet is told what it follows (pose, depth, tile or lineart). A ControlNet not scanned yet goes by the folder it is in (SDXL_1.0, NoobAI…). When a block lacks a model, Anomalous reads ComfyUI's file lists anew once before saying so: ComfyUI does not see a model just downloaded into a subfolder until it restarts.
  - The upscales take the least enlarging upscale model that reaches their size (a 2× one for 1.25×, less work than a 4× one scaled back down).
  - A way is built for the canvas's model: on an Anima model, Follow a layout and Follow a pose use Anima's ControlNet-LLLite, and Repaint: crop and stitch adds Anima's repaint LLLite; the AI still only names the way. Needs ComfyUI-Anima-LLLite and anima-lllite models in models/controlnet. Anima's LLLite steers more gently than an SDXL ControlNet, so the prompt has to agree with the picture, and the AI is told so.
  - With several samplers on the canvas the AI has to say which one; nothing is guessed.
- A **Ways** panel on the right of the AI workbench lists every way by job, what it does and where it came from. Ways missing a node pack or a model are greyed with what they lack; a missing pack opens ComfyUI-Manager searched for it. Each has a switch: one turned off is not offered to the AI, and building it is refused. When it begins, the AI gets only the ways that are on and can run here, with one line on what installing which pack would add.
- The Ways panel says how each way was tried: the card (so far an RTX 4050 laptop, 6 GB), the size, a whole run's time; its settings are for that card (the upscale stops at 1.25×). The AI hears it too when it uses one, so it can say how long to wait.
- Anomalous keeps a list of the models the ways use (api/block_models.json): each one's links (Hugging Face, Civitai, GitHub), size, SHA256, licence and folder. Each way names the models it recommends; the panel ticks the ones here (renamed ones too) and downloads a missing one with Anomalous's downloader in a click, checking its hash. - 图跑完后，第一个告诉 AI "跑完了" 的回复（不管是查进度还是别的工具）直接带着这次出的图，最多 4 张缩略图，每次运行只带一次。AI 不用再去取图，看完就能留言。
- The answer that first tells the AI its run ended, run_status or any other, carries up to four thumbnails of the run's pictures, once per run, so it need not fetch them with get_image_info.
- 跑完叫我：关卡窗口新增“等跑图”，默认“跑完叫我”。AI 提交后最多等 1 分钟（跑得快的当场就拿到结果），还没跑完就告诉你在跑，然后收工，不再一遍遍查进度（以前一张图要查 10 次左右）。跑完后，留言框上方有几句话，点一下复制，粘到 AI 的对话框里，它就接着看结果和图；AI 知道结果后这几句话自动消失。选“AI 自己等”就和以前一样。
- Tell AI when done: a new setting in the Gates window, on by default. The AI waits up to a minute after queueing (a quick run comes back with its pictures at once) and, if the run still goes, says so and ends its turn instead of polling run_status. When the run ends, lines over the message box are ready to copy into the AI's chat; they go once the AI knows. "AI waits" keeps the old way.
- 工具说明压短：常用工具的说明重写，去掉重复和绕的话，规则和“不要做”的都保留；开始任务时收到的规则从约 1900 字减到约 900 字。
- Shorter tool descriptions: the everyday tools' descriptions are rewritten without repeats, keeping their rules; begin_task's rules are about 900 characters instead of 1,900.
- 工具目录：AI 开始任务时，回复里附带一张按“想做的事”排好的工具目录（出图、画布、模型、提示词、图片、和你沟通……），AI 看一眼就直接去用对的工具，不用先到处试。目录从工具登记表自动生成，只列当前给 AI 的工具，新工具会自己出现。
- A directory of the tools by what the AI wants to do comes with begin_task's answer (and get_situation's while there is no task): each tool offered under its stage of the work with its title, built from the catalog so new tools show up by themselves.
- 给 AI 的话常驻在消息框上方：AI 停下来的时候（没在调用工具、没在跑图、没在等你回答），AI 工作台消息框上方总有一行话，点一下就复制，粘到 AI 的对话框里。话跟着情况变：图跑完了是“跑完了，看看结果”这几句；你在 AMB 里回复了、AI 还没收到，是“我在 Anomalous 里回复了，看看再接着做”；别的时候是“继续”。最后一句“用 Anomalous 接着做任务：…”给新开的 AI 对话用。原来挂在跑图那一步下面的几句话挪到了这里。“交给 AI”给你的那句话也不再叫 AI 用旧的 show_panel 问你。
- Lines for the AI's chat stay over the message box: whenever the AI is not in a call, a run or a question, the AI workbench offers a line to copy that fits what waits (about the run that ended; "I replied in Anomalous, take a look and go on" when it has not got your reply yet; else "Go on"), and one that picks the task up in a new chat. The lines under a finished run's step moved here. The Hand to AI sentence no longer tells the AI to ask with show_panel.
- AI 在 AMB 里问你时至少等 30 秒：就算它自己说不等（Gemini 常这样），你在 AMB 里点了选项，它在同一步里就收到，接着做，不用再回 AI 的对话框说“继续”。“加节点”工具的说明也提醒 AI：要加一组互相连着的节点（重绘、放大一段），用一次调用的“加一组节点”。
- ask_user waits at least 30 s even when the AI asks it not to, so an answer given in Anomalous right away reaches the same call and the AI goes on without a word in its chat. add_node's description points to add_nodes for several nodes wired to each other (an inpaint or upscale branch).
- 时间线更短：AI 连着做的事（查资料、加节点、连线、写提示词、改参数、记决策）三步以上、其中至少两步不是查资料时，叠成一行“做了 N 步”，下面一排小标签写做了什么（加了节点 ×5、连了线 ×10……），点开就是原来的每一步。行上有“撤销这一串”，一下撤掉这一串在画布上的改动。你的话、AI 留言、问你的、面板、出图、出错的、等你允许的、正在做的一直露在外面。AI 还在做时，这一行写着最后一步是什么。一个修手的任务从 43 行变成 18 行。
- A shorter timeline: three or more of the AI's working steps in a row (reads, canvas changes, prompts, settings, decisions; two of them not reads) fold into one row, "Did N steps", with chips of what they did and "Undo these" for their canvas changes; it opens to the steps as before. Your words, the AI's notes and questions, panels, runs, errors, steps waiting for you and running ones stay in sight. While the AI is at it, the row names its last step. A hand-fix task went from 43 rows to 18.
- 展台自动打开：AI 往展台放了新面板，右栏自己打开并跳到它，你关掉了或者在看节点结构、决策、素材也一样；AI 只是改已有的面板（比如进度）时不会。展台顶上有开关“有新东西就打开”，默认开着。窗口窄时不抢时间线，只在“展台”标签上亮个点；AI 问你问题时仍然切过去。
- The stage opens by itself: a new panel from the AI opens the side column at it, even when you closed it or were on another view; the AI changing a panel (a progress bar) does not. The switch "Open on new panels" in the stage's head turns it off. In a narrow window it lights the stage's tab instead of taking the timeline's place; a question still brings the stage forward.
- 打开右栏的按钮更醒目：时间线里每个面板那一行变成卡片，左边小图标表示里面是什么（问题、图、对比、节点结构），标题下一行写内容（“1 张图”“问你 · 2 个选项”），右边是实心按钮“在右侧打开”；右栏正在显示的那个面板，按钮换成绿点“正在右侧显示”。跑图那一步的“在右侧看大图”也换成同样的按钮。“在画布上找到”“撤销”“在图库里看”这些蓝色小字换成带图标的小按钮。
- Ways to the right are easier to see: each panel's line in the timeline is a card with an icon of what it holds, a line of what is in it ("1 picture", "Asks you · 2 options") and a solid "Show on the right" button, which turns into a green "On the right now" while that panel is in view there. A run's "See it large on the right" is the same button. "Find on canvas", "Undo" and "See it in the gallery" are small buttons with icons instead of blue text.

- 修复任务画布标签关闭或切换失败后误用当前工作流的问题：操作会明确报错，重新打开或明确指定新画布后才能继续。下载缺失模型现在也跟随任务画布；留言里的 `@` 搭配和工作流按完整名称查找，不再因模糊搜索前几项占满而误报缺失。
- Task canvas actions now fail when their recorded tab is closed or cannot be activated instead of using another workflow. Missing-model downloads follow the task tab, and message references resolve exact combo/workflow names before search limits.

- AI 工作台任务工具栏新增小巧的“运行状态”按钮与浮窗（移除遮挡时间线的常驻大横条）：点开即可查看真实运行状态、排队、节点进度、用时和结果，运行中直接在按钮上显示进度百分比；没有收到运行信息时明确提示，不再空转显示“AI 正在工作”或把沉默当成完成。页面可见时自动补查状态。运行工具默认等待 15 秒并返回实际结果；外部提交可用 `track_run` 接入准确的运行 ID，不会重新出图。
- The AI workbench now places confirmed execution state, queue/node progress, elapsed time and results inside a compact toolbar button and popover (instead of a tall sticky banner), with visible-page refresh. Untracked work and silence are explicit. Run calls wait briefly for real results; `track_run` attaches external submission IDs without resubmitting.

### 右边一栏一次一样，按钮固定在右上角 / One view at a time on the right, switched from the top right
- 工作台右边那一栏一次只显示一样东西，用窗口右上角的三个按钮切换：**展台**、**节点结构**（AI 跑过的节点结构图，大图，可以选看哪一次运行）、**素材**（原来停在屏幕右边的素材小窗，现在就在这一栏里）。再点一下亮着的按钮收起这一栏；切走再切回来，内容都还在。时间线里每次运行都有 **在右侧看大图**。
- 任务标题和那一排按钮在时间线往下翻时一直停在顶上。
- 右边这一栏改成一张单独的卡片，不再用一条线硬隔开；默认窄一些，给中间的时间线留出地方。它左边的缝里有三个小点，鼠标移上去变成一条竖线，拖动调宽窄（记得住，双击恢复）。这条缝和时间线的滚动条分开，不会再一抓就抓错。
- 左边的任务列表用窗口左上角的按钮收起 / 展开；收起后鼠标移到按钮上，列表会临时浮出来，点任务或移开就收回。
- 素材在右边这一栏里时，鼠标移过卡片不再自动跳进详细说明（之前很难操作），点卡片上的 ⓘ 才看。
- 左边任务列表收起后，鼠标从按钮往下移去点任务时列表不会再中途消失（之前移得慢一点就会收回）：鼠标在按钮和列表围成的范围里都算还在用，离开后才收回。
- The folded task list no longer vanishes on the way down from its button: it stays while the pointer is anywhere in the box around the button and the list, however slowly it moves.
- **模型补丁不再被当成 ControlNet**：Anima LLLite、Qwen-Image 分块 ControlNet、Z-Image Fun ControlNet 这类文件名字里常带 ControlNet，但只有「加载模型补丁」节点能读，它只看 `model_patches` 文件夹。现在拖进模型页会自动放进 `model_patches`；已经放在 `controlnet` 里的，模型页的“整理”会提示挪过去。
- Model patches (Anima LLLite, the Qwen-Image block-wise and Z-Image Fun ControlNets) are told from ControlNets by their header: imported into `model_patches`, and Tidy offers to move those already in `controlnet`.
- **AI 找模型时直接知道该用哪个节点**：`search_models` / `get_model` 给每个模型写上能加载它的节点；放错文件夹的会说该放哪、放在这里加载不了，同名文件已经在对的地方时会直接指给 AI。新工具 `move_model` 把放错的模型挪过去（新关卡“挪动模型文件”，默认先问你；从不覆盖文件）。AI 不必再去命令行翻 ComfyUI 的源码。AI 加节点时如果下拉列表是空的，会被告知“ComfyUI 没找到文件，可能放错了文件夹”。
- search_models and get_model say which node types load each model, and where a misfiled one belongs; `move_model` moves it there behind a new "files" gate (asks by default, never overwrites). An empty drop-down in add_node now says ComfyUI found no file for it.
- AI 第一次在任务的副本标签页上动手时，切换标签页不再被记成“你在画布上的改动”告诉 AI。
- The AI switching to its task tab is no longer reported to it as a canvas change of the user's.
- **工作台里直接用图库的图**：留言框左边新增图片按钮，打开图库（最新的在前，可按提示词、模型、seed 搜索），点选几张后 **加进留言**；打 **@** 时也能看到图库最新的图，打字会顺带在图库里搜。AI 收到的是图片本身和它的生成参数。
- Gallery pictures in the AI workbench: the message box's picture button picks them from the gallery (newest first, searchable), and "@" offers the gallery's newest pictures and searches it as you type; the AI gets each picture with what made it.
- **等 AI 接手时先备好素材**：“等 AI 接手”的任务是一页：最上面是 **把这句话发给你的 AI** 和 **复制这句话**，往下滚时它停在顶上，挑完图不用翻回去；下面的 **写一句要做什么**、**从图库挑图给 AI**（大图库可搜索，点一张就放到最下面的留言框，再点一下拿掉）都是可选的，点了才展开，行上写着已经放了几张；备好的都列在下面。AI 接手时一起收到。那句话改成让 AI 照任务里的交代做，没有交代才问你，不会一接手就停下来问。
- A task waiting for an AI is one page: **Send this to your AI** with **Copy** leads and stays at the top while the page scrolls; **Say what to do** and **Pictures from the gallery** (a big searchable grid feeding the message box) are optional folds that open when pressed and say how many pictures are in; what is ready is listed under them. The sentence now has the AI do what the task says, asking only when it says nothing.
- **不全屏时中间更宽敞**：AI 工作台的窗口不够高时（没有最大化），任务标题、状态、时间步数、“接着”和右边的工具并成一行，上下留白也收紧；输入框下面的提示只在出错时显示（平时鼠标停在输入框上能看到）；等 AI 接手的页面图片变小、步骤靠紧。同样的窗口里，中间能多显示约两成内容；最大化时样子不变。
- In a low window (not maximised) the AI workbench keeps the task's head to one line, tightens the room around it, shows the message box's hint only for a failure (its words are the input's tooltip) and shrinks the waiting page's picture: about a fifth more room for the timeline.
- **换类型**：模型详情页多了 **换类型** 按钮，给“导入时类型选错了 / 代码认不出、放错了文件夹”的模型用。窗口里写明现在放在哪一类、文件头看起来像什么（看得出就替你选好并标“推荐”，不确定标“可能是”，看不出就不猜），每一类都写着用哪个节点加载；可以改挪到的根目录和子文件夹，下面显示完整路径。挪动和“整理”用的是同一套：封面、信息、备注一起走，从不覆盖文件，挪完 ComfyUI 的模型列表马上更新。导入单个模型后，提示里也有 **类型选错了？**，点一下直接改。
- "Change type" on a model's page, and "Wrong type?" in the toast after importing one model: the window says where it is and what its header suggests (picked and marked when it can tell, nothing guessed when it cannot), offers every type with its loader and where it goes, and moves it as Tidy does.
- 跨盘挪动、复制模型时，除了比大小，还会比对文件开头、中间、结尾各 1 MB 是否一模一样，对不上就不删原文件（只多花几毫秒，不需要事先有指纹）。
- A model copied to another drive is checked at its start, middle and end (a megabyte each) besides its size before the original goes; no fingerprint needed, no felt cost.
- 挪动、导入模型时，就算目标位置在最后一刻冒出一个同名文件，也绝不会被盖掉：这次挪动会失败，两边的文件都原样保留（之前在 Windows 上理论上有极小的机会覆盖）。
- Moving or importing a model never replaces a file that took the name at the last moment: the move fails and both files stay (on Windows a rename could replace it before).
- **AI 能放大看图、看预览，不用再自己写代码**：新工具 `zoom_image` 把一张图的某一块放大给 AI 看，还能把两张图的同一块并排比较（改之前和改之后）；输出、输入、临时预览的图都能看，只看不改。出图结果里会列出这次运行的预览图（比如 OpenPose 骨骼、遮罩的预览），AI 用它们就能检查中间结果，不必自己去跑预处理器。AI 开任务、收到遮罩时都会被明确告知：要改图就跑 ComfyUI 的工作流，绝不用自己的代码去画、去改、去覆盖图片文件。
- zoom_image shows part of a picture larger (output, input or temp folder), with the same part of a second picture to compare, reading only; run_status lists a run's PreviewImage pictures; begin_task and the repaint note tell the AI to change pictures only by running workflows, never by editing picture files with its own code.
- **AI 能直接看输入文件夹里的图**：`get_image_info` 加了 `folder=input`，能看 ComfyUI 输入文件夹里的图（你标注时 AMB 存的重绘遮罩图就在那里）：尺寸、要重画的区域在哪、占多大，也能附上图本身。交给 AI 遮罩图时也明说“它已经在输入文件夹里了，不用跑命令检查”，Gemini 不会再为这个弹窗问你。
- get_image_info with folder=input reads a picture in ComfyUI's input folder (the inpaint picture Anomalous writes for a repaint): its size and the area LoadImage gives as MASK, optionally attached; the marks message says the inpaint picture is in place, so the AI need not check it with a command.
- **AI 少去翻文件**：AMB 工具返回给 AI 的内容去掉了多余的空格、画布里 ComfyUI 自己的内部字段不再列出、操作记录默认只给最近 10 条，这样 Antigravity 不必把结果另存成文件再让 Gemini 去读。AI 开始任务时也会被告知：模型、图片、节点、画布和运行都有 AMB 的工具，不要自己去读 ComfyUI 的文件夹、源码或接口（那样每一步都要你点允许）。
- Tool results reach the AI as compact JSON, without the canvas's internal "$$" widgets, and recent_activity gives the last 10 by default, so apps such as Antigravity keep more of them in the chat instead of a file the AI must open; begin_task tells the AI that AMB's tools cover models, pictures, nodes, canvas and runs, so it need not read ComfyUI's folders, source or routes.
- 修复输入框上方的出图进度停在“节点 0/29 0%”不动的问题：现在它和时间线里那一步、标题栏的进度按钮一起更新。
- The run line over the message box now follows the run's progress with the timeline's bar and the header's button, instead of staying at the start.
- **最下面就能看到 AI 在做什么、要你做什么**：输入框上方多了一条“现在”栏：AI 出图时显示进度（节点、步数、百分比、进度条）和停止按钮；AI 某一步还在进行时说在做什么；AI 想做要你点头的事（比如挪模型、下载）时，直接在这里 **允许 / 不要**；AI 问你问题时，问题和选项就在这里，点一下就回答（有推荐的带 ☆，也能自己写一句或跳过），多选和挑图会带你去展台。
- A "now" strip over the message box: a run's progress with stop, the AI's step still going, a gate to allow or refuse right there, and the AI's open question with its options as buttons (one's own words or skip too; several picks and pictures to pick lead to the stage).
- **拖进来、粘贴进来的图片也能发给 AI**：把电脑里的图片拖到输入框上，或者直接 Ctrl+V 粘贴截图，它会存进 ComfyUI 输出文件夹里的 `ai_inbox`（图库里也看得到，从不覆盖文件，只收 PNG、JPEG、WebP、GIF，最大 25 MB），并加到这条消息上。从 ComfyUI 页面里拖来的输出图片直接加上，不另存。
- Pictures dropped on the message box or pasted are kept in the output folder's `ai_inbox` (seen in the gallery; never over a file; PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 25 MB) and attached; an output picture dragged from ComfyUI's page is attached as it is.
- **很长的 AI 留言先折叠**：超过八行左右的留言只显示开头，点 **展开全文** 看全部。
- Long AI notes are folded to their first lines with "Show all".
- **输入框固定在最下面，像聊天软件一样**：不管时间线多长、翻到哪里，留言框都停在任务这一栏的最下方。它上面是这条消息要带的东西：图片显示成小图，每张有 **✎ 标注**（画框、圈、写要改哪里，标好后单独发给 AI）和 **×**；@ 提到的模型、搭配显示成小标签。从图库挑的图不再往文字里塞“@文件名”。左边是 **图库** 和 **素材** 两个按钮，给了东西就亮起并显示数量。输入框会随内容变高：Enter 发送，Shift+Enter 换行；只发图片不写字也可以。往上翻了一屏以上，或者往上翻着看时 AI 有了新步骤，输入框右上方会出现圆形 **↓** 按钮（有新步骤时角上带数字），点一下回到最新；自己发出的消息会自动滚到眼前。时间线里你发的图片和 AI 出的图一样，可以查看、对比、收藏、标注。
- The message box is docked at the foot of the task column like a chat app's: over the input what the message carries (pictures with ✎ to mark up and ×, chips for what "@" names; gallery pictures are attached, not typed in), beside it the gallery and materials buttons with their counts, an input that grows (Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line; pictures alone are fine), a round ↓ button back to the newest step (with the count of new ones) when you read more than a screen up or the AI adds steps meanwhile, and your own message scrolled into view. Pictures you sent carry the same tools as the AI's. The side column's grip sits in its own gap, three dots, beside the timeline's scrollbar instead of over it.

- run_status before any run answers state "none" instead of failing, so the task no longer shows a failed step for it.
- **宽窗口里时间线收在中间**：右边一栏关着、窗口又很宽时，时间线、标题和输入框不再横跨整个窗口，而是收成中间约 880 像素宽的一列（像聊天软件那样），滚动条仍在最右边。时间线里的图片按自己的大小从左排开，一张图不再被撑成横跨整行的大灰框。

The size goes to the latent a run starts from, never to a hires fix's latent upscale. With blanks a run asks as the stricter of the GPU's and the canvas's gate.

- **AI 每连一根线都知道还缺什么**：`add_node`、`connect_nodes`、`remove_nodes` 的回复里附上画布上还没接的输入，以及哪些现有输出能接上，像一张清单，照着补完就能跑。
- add_node, connect_nodes and remove_nodes answer with the inputs still unwired and what fits them.

- Pictures changed outside Anomalous show in the timeline: when the AI calls, Anomalous looks at the top of the output and input folders for pictures no run saved and pictures rewritten in place since its previous call, and tells the AI with its answer; new pictures wait while ComfyUI runs a prompt. `log_outside` is the AI's own word on a command or script it ran. Both are "Outside AMB" steps, counted on the task.

### 运行时看得更清楚，也能一键停 / See more of a run, and stop it in one click
- AI 运行工作流时，进度条旁边多了红色的 **■ 停止**（任务工具栏的运行状态按钮旁边也有）：一点就停下这一次运行，排队中的移出队列、正在跑的立刻中断，只动这次运行。时间线上记着“你停止了运行”，AI 也会收到，不会自己再跑一遍；AI 自己也能用新工具 `stop_run` 停掉自己的运行。
- 进度条下面多了当前节点**约剩多久**、每步几秒、**显存还剩多少**，以及 ComfyUI **控制台**最新的一行（比如放大模型加载、`Model Initializing ...`），点开看最近几行。AI 收到的进度里也有这些，不用再去翻控制台。
- 出图一排上队，AMB 就读这次工作流，算出每个采样器要在多大的图上采样。放大模型放大后又整张重新采样、尺寸超出这张显卡的舒适范围、要解码特别大的图时，会出现黄色提醒，说清可能很慢或爆显存，以及怎么改（只想放大就单用放大模型，或先缩小、减步数、换分块解码）。AI 收到同样的提醒，并被要求先问你要不要停。

Runs show time left and per step, VRAM, and ComfyUI's console (the bar's note, models loading), for the user and the AI alike. Once queued, each sampler's working size is estimated from the prompt, with warnings for an upscaled picture sampled again, a size past the card or a huge decode. Esc closes the run-status card only.

### AI 在画布上搭工作流 / The AI builds workflows on the canvas
- AI 可以在你眼前的画布上搭工作流了：查这台 ComfyUI 有哪些节点（包括自定义节点）、每个节点有哪些输入输出和可选项；新建一个工作流标签页（空白的，或者一次搭好整套并自动排好位置，不动你正在开的那个）；加节点、连线、改参数、删节点。每一步都在画布上当场发生，是一次 Ctrl+Z，记在操作记录里，并在 AI 工作台那一步下面显示改了什么，连线也会写成“某节点的输入 model：（空）→ 加载模型 #4 · MODEL”。连不上的类型、不存在的选项、写错的参数会先被拦下并告诉 AI 正确的写法，画布不会留下半截。新建的标签页名字带时间，不会打开或覆盖你存过的工作流。连线会绕成死循环时也会先拦下。运行被 ComfyUI 拒绝时，AI 收到的是 ComfyUI 给的具体原因（哪个节点、什么问题），不再只有一句“运行失败”。

Each is one Ctrl+Z step, logged as the AI's and shown under its AI workbench step, rewiring included. Wrong types, choices and settings are refused with what fits, before anything changes. Wiring that would loop is refused too, and a run ComfyUI rejects reports ComfyUI's own reason.

### AI 后端修复 / AI backend fixes
- 出图结果按本次提交返回的准确运行 ID 归属，不再通过全局队列差集猜测；其他 AI、其他页面或后续手动排队不会混入。批次只提交成功一部分时，保留这些运行的进度和图片，并明确报告未全部提交；不会自动重跑整批。旧页面需刷新后才能接手出图，不支持请求 ID 的旧版 ComfyUI 前端会在提交前提示更新。同一页面正在提交时另一 AI 收到忙碌提示，待提交结束即可重试。
- Workflow runs now follow exact submission IDs in batch order. Partial submissions keep accepted runs/results and report the missing portion; no automatic resubmission. Stale pages cannot claim runs until refreshed, unsupported host frontends fail before queueing, and a second AI waits while the same page is submitting. Native widget/seed behavior is preserved and later manual batches do not delay the AI receipt.
- “允许一次”只用于一次调用，不会被相同参数的后续调用重复使用；无法建立确认记录时不会直接执行。修复确认超时后用户刚好回答、工具返回又覆盖答案的问题；重启后的待确认步骤仍能把后续答案交给 AI，回答也会唤醒正在等待的 AI。
- 指定任务或会话读取留言时，不再取走同一 AI 客户端其他任务的留言、标记和回答。
- 没找到排队记录、无法读取运行记录、跟踪超时均明确标为未完成，不再误报完成或永久显示运行中；批次内出错及时记录，批次进度保留总数。跟踪停止不会取消 ComfyUI 实际运行。
- 标图的校验和保存移到工作线程；跨盘图片路径返回校验错误，错误类型的 MCP 工具名返回协议错误。
- One-shot approvals cannot be reused, missing approval records fail closed, and timeout/restart races preserve user decisions and wake waiting readers. Task/session input stays isolated. Missing or unreadable run history and monitoring timeouts no longer imply success or leave receipts running forever. Marked-image validation/storage runs off the event loop; malformed image paths and tool names return controlled errors.

### ✨ AI workbench: see everything the AI did, open it, undo it (AI 工作台)
- **A new page on the rail, *AI workbench***: every step an AI app (Claude Code, Cursor, Cherry Studio… connected through Anomalous's MCP) takes is kept, grouped into **tasks**. The AI names a task when it starts (a new `begin_task` tool); otherwise one app's calls that follow each other make one task.

The cloud guide ends with a section for AI assistants (what is going on, the steps, how to check, what to do when it did not work, what to tell the user about safety), so a user can hand the link to their AI.
- On a rented cloud server (AutoDL, RunPod…) the page is always opened from another device, so since v1.58.1 the plugin only let it look: scans, downloads, moves and saves were refused. Now there are two ways: open ComfyUI through an **SSH tunnel** (the address starts with 127.0.0.1; it counts as the computer ComfyUI runs on, nothing to change), or make an empty file named **`allow_remote_changes`** in the plugin folder on the server and restart ComfyUI. With the file, every device that can open the page may change things; the page can neither make nor remove it, and the MCP endpoint stays for this computer only. How, and the risk: [docs/guides/cloud-server.md](docs/guides/cloud-server.md).
- The **View only** chip beside the page title can be pressed: it says what is refused and the two ways above, with a **Copy** button for the one line to paste into the server's terminal (the plugin's real path on that server already filled in), so there is no folder to find. With the file there it reads **Remote changes on**, in yellow, and its dialog gives the line that turns it off again.
- **Models that are links (模型是快捷方式/链接时)**: on a cloud server such as AutoDL every model in the models folder is a link into the platform's shared model disk. Scans wrote the info beside the link, but the cards read it from the disk behind it: a scanned model showed as not scanned, under the disk's file name (`6b4679…`) instead of its own, and editing, deleting, opening its folder or finding it from a saved workflow could be refused as "outside the models folder". A linked model is now taken as named, with its covers and info beside the link; edits, renames and deletes act on the link, never on the shared disk; moving one in Tidy moves the link instead of copying the model disk's file onto the server. Names leaving the folder (`..`) are refused as before.
- **Models linked from another tool keep their info**: a model that is a link with no info beside it shows the info beside the file it links to (A1111's Civitai Helper leaves `.civitai.info` there). Only read there; covers, edits and scans stay beside the link.

### 🌐 A proxy for the plugin, and why Civitai cannot be reached (代理设置与连不上的原因)

The README's backup note no longer calls these features a test.
- **Model folders, readable (模型文件夹设置更好懂)**: folder management is a view of the settings page instead of a pop-up. Grouping is two plain choices, **By model type** or **By folders on disk** (it was "Category mode / Physical mode"); the types shown on the Models page come first, each with its usual name (Checkpoint, Diffusion…) and folder name, a switch and ↑↓ to order them; the ones that are off follow, and the many folders ComfyUI registers that few people use (configs, datasets…) fold away. Each change applies at once; there is no Save and reload button any more.
- **Up-to-date guides (说明跟上新界面)**: the **!** "What's new" guide and the README now describe the rail, Home, keeping results, Prompt Studio, Model Check and character voices instead of the old tabs and bottom bar.
- **The tour follows how you work (导览按使用顺序讲)**: the interface tour now goes the way the pages are used: the rail as one line (pick models, keep a good result, use it again), the Gallery's ☆ and where each kind of keep goes, Workflows (with share codes), Combos, Prompt Studio, Current node, then scanning, Model Check, the **!** guide and Settings. Steps that still described translating on Current node and the old scan wizard are rewritten, and the tour's text follows the interface language like the rest of the browser.

### ⭐ Keep and use again (收藏与复用)
- **Keep a result from the gallery (图库里点 ☆ 收藏)**: every image in the gallery has a ☆. It asks what to keep, and says where each goes: **The whole workflow** (a Workflow Recipe on the Workflows page, with the picture as its cover), **Its combo** (the main model, LoRAs and positive prompt, on the Combos page, ready to put on the canvas; models this computer does not have are left out and the message says how many) or **Just the prompts** (positive and negative, in your saved prompts). No name is asked: it is named after the prompt. Kept images show ★; a row already kept shows ✓ and opens it, and its **Remove** (取消收藏) moves what was kept to the Recycle Bin after asking, so the star goes back to ☆. The image workbench's **Keep** opens the same menu (its name-and-tags form is gone). The Material Library's Recent shelf, which did the same for whole workflows only, is gone: the gallery lists the same images. Deleting an image from the gallery asks in the usual dialog instead of over the picture.
- **Whole workflows live on the Workflows page (完整工作流都在“工作流”页)**: a result kept whole (now with the gallery's ☆, or from the image workbench) is a Workflow Recipe with that picture as its cover (it was a material), so versions, parameter sets and Model Check work on it. The Material Library keeps the pieces (nodes, parameters, prompts) and lost its Workflow category. Whole workflows kept as materials before show on the Workflows page as "N more in the library — Move them here"; moving saves each as a recipe and leaves the material file where it is, just no longer listed. Recipes that are not whole workflows are called Modules everywhere (the filter said "Sub-graph").
- **Prompt Notes are now Combos, a page of their own (提示词笔记改名“搭配”，有了自己的页面)**: a note was always a main model, a few LoRAs and a prompt kept together, so it is called a combo and sits on the rail as **Combos**, with a card on Home. The page shows every combo as a card with its model's cover, its LoRAs and the start of its prompt, a search box and **New combo**; a card opens the combo to edit, and **‹ All combos** goes back. The old notes column with its quick-jump list is gone. Your notes are the same files and show up as combos; prompts saved from notes before still show in the Material Library and can be saved as a new combo.
- **Put a combo on the canvas (搭配一键放到画布)**: **Put on canvas** on a combo's card or in its editor (formerly Send to canvas, under More) lays the combo out as a group of nodes wired together: its main model loader, its LoRAs and a positive and a negative prompt node; the group follows the pointer until you click, and Esc takes it off again (nothing is logged or left behind). The positive node holds the combo's prompt (left empty when it has none, rather than leaving the prompt nodes out) and the negative node gets Prompt Studio's common negative card. A combo card can also be dragged onto the canvas, and the group is built where it is dropped. Nothing already on the canvas is changed (to change an existing node's values, use Current node). A new group from a UNet combo connects its prompt nodes to the canvas's CLIP loader when there is exactly one, and says so once the group is down (the message no longer pops up while the group still follows the pointer, where closing it put the group down).
- **Recipe detail, plainer (配方详情更清爽)**: the models a recipe uses are one list: preview, name, the node, whether this computer has it under the saved name, size, your note and its download page. The recipe's own "match local model / apply match" buttons, the identity badges, the advanced blocks with paths and hashes, the origin editor and the recipe fingerprint are gone: when the workflow opens, Model Check finds renamed or moved models and puts them in, and saving the recipe again keeps that (the old version is kept). The status says "Every model is on this computer" or "N models missing" with what happens next. The save dialog and the empty page speak plainly (no more "unclosed node chain" or "self-healing"), and the parameters page lost its save-to-library button on every node (pick nodes with "select some parameters" instead).
- **Shared workflows say where their models come from (分享的工作流带上模型来源)**: saving a workflow or an image now also stores each model's download link (the one you set in the model editor, else its Civitai page) unless the workflow already keeps one, and drops links of models it no longer uses, so after Model Check swaps in your own model, what you export names your file, fingerprint and link while the workflow you opened stays as it was. GGUF, .pth and .sft models get fingerprints too. The share-code export says how many models carry a fingerprint, warns when some have none, and can leave the links out.

### ✍️ Prompts and Current node (提示词与当前节点)
- **Prompt Studio edits the node itself (提示词工坊直接改节点)**: no more assembly board, "write to node" step or positive / negative tabs. Select a prompt node and its prompt boxes appear in the studio, positive and negative together, as tags: click one to make it stronger or weaker, double-click to edit it, drag to reorder, × to remove. Every change goes straight into the node, and the studio's **Undo** (or Ctrl+Z on the canvas) takes it back; line breaks in your prompt stay where they were. Type in a box and press Enter to add tags: Chinese, Japanese, Korean or any other language is translated to English first, and tags the box has already are not added twice; a box that holds words that are not English offers to translate them. The meaning menu in the top bar puts each tag's meaning under it in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish, French, German or Portuguese; **Text** shows the whole text instead. With no prompt node selected you write a draft, which can be saved or dragged onto the canvas by its ⠿ handle; the studio keeps editing the last prompt node until you select another one or press the ✕ beside its name. A clicked card goes into the box of its role (a negative card never lands in a positive box). The six starter cards are down to three (quality, common negative, hands and anatomy), your saved prompts are under **My prompts** with their first words showing, and the studio no longer reads every saved prompt again every 30 seconds, only new ones.
- **Saved prompts are managed in Prompt Studio (存过的提示词在提示词工坊里管理)**: the studio's library cards for prompts you saved used to be read-only ("managed by the source material"). Their preview now has **Rename** and **Delete** (the whole saved prompt, positive and negative together, to the Recycle Bin), and any card can be dragged out of the studio onto the canvas: onto a prompt box it fills that box (outlined by role, with the text previewed before you let go, one Ctrl+Z or Undo in the message to take it back), onto empty canvas it makes a prompt node.
- **Prompt boxes found by what they are (提示词框按节点定义识别)**: dropping or writing a prompt (material library, Prompt Studio, translator) now finds a node's prompt boxes by ComfyUI's own definition (multiline text inputs) instead of guessing from names, so a filename or "value" box no longer takes a prompt and third-party prompt nodes are recognised. Whether a box is positive or negative follows its wiring to the sampler, not the node's title: a CLIP Text Encode connected to the negative input gets the negative prompt even if it is still called "CLIP Text Encode". Prompt Studio's "take prompts from the selected node" also reads the roles right again (it could not follow links in current ComfyUI).
- **See where a dragged prompt goes (拖提示词前先看清楚落在哪)**: while you drag a prompt from the material library, every prompt box on the canvas is outlined (green for positive, red for negative, grey when it is not wired to a sampler) and the box under the pointer lights up. The label next to the pointer says exactly what releasing does, e.g. "Release: into #6's positive prompt box; the negative prompt box on the same sampler (#7) too". Drop on a box and that box is filled; the matching negative (or positive) box of the same sampler gets the other half of the material. On a node with several prompt boxes, point at the one you mean instead of letting it guess. A short message confirms what was filled; the library's receipt undoes it. Dropping a prompt plan on empty canvas used to create an empty prompt node; it now creates a positive and, when the plan has one, a negative node with their text.
- **See it before you let go (拖的时候先看到结果)**: dragging a prompt over a prompt box shows, in that box and in the opposite box on the same sampler, the text releasing would put there; move away and they go back. Dragging saved values over a node of their kind lists what would change, such as "steps 12 → 28, cfg 4.5 → 5.5", or says nothing would change.
- **Undo what you dropped (拖错了可以撤回)**: anything Anomalous writes on the canvas (a material dropped on a node, a prompt put in a box, values applied in Current node, a model swapped or a LoRA inserted, a recipe or combo sent to the canvas) is now one step of ComfyUI's own **Ctrl+Z**; before, a drop was not, and Ctrl+Z could undo the step before it instead. After a drop, the message at the top right has **Undo** and stays while the pointer is on it. Dropping a prompt on empty canvas can be undone the same way. In **Activity**, canvas changes made since the page was opened have **Undo** while nothing has changed them since; entries that removed nodes or rewired existing ones only record.
- **Current node (当前节点, formerly Node Assistant)**: the panel no longer splits into "actions" and "parameter sets" tabs; it is one page with the model actions on top and **Parameters** below. Parameters lists every saved set of values for this kind of node in one list — from the material library and from recipes' parameter sets, the same values saved twice shown once — and each entry says exactly what it would change on the selected node ("steps: 20 → 30"). **Apply these N** changes only those; seeds and model files stay (change the model above), and the receipt undoes it. **Save these values** keeps the node's current values in the material library, so the next node of that kind can use them.
- **Prompts on Current node (当前节点列出提示词框)**: Current node now lists the selected node's prompt boxes, each marked positive or negative by its wiring, and **Edit in Prompt Studio** opens them in the studio, where they are edited and translated. The separate Prompt Translator window is gone.

### 🩺 Model Check (模型检查)
- **Model Check, formerly Model Doctor, redone (模型检查，原“模型医生”，重做)**: when a workflow opens with models this computer does not have, a bar over the canvas says how many, and how many are here under another name or folder; **Put in** swaps those in and **Show** opens the doctor. The doctor page lists what needs you first and says why in plain words (same file found elsewhere, same size only, not on this computer, no fingerprint saved in the workflow), with a button for each: put in, use it, pick one yourself, or look it up on Civitai; the models that are fine fold away. Opening the doctor no longer changes the workflow by itself, and a model put in by the doctor or its picker no longer stays marked red by ComfyUI. The "auto-detect on startup" switch (it did nothing) and the doctor's own deep hash scan are gone: the check hashes same-size files itself, and the page points to the scan page when unscanned models could be the missing ones.
- **The Model Check bar can be turned off (模型检查横条可以关掉)**: the bar now starts with "Model Check:" so it is clear it comes from the browser, not ComfyUI, and has **Don't show again**, which stops checking workflows when they open and says where to turn it back on. The same switch is in Settings → Workflows ("Check for missing models when a workflow opens", on by default); with it off, run Model Check from the rail when you want it.
- **A model put in by Model Check stays put (模型检查换上的模型不会再变回去)**: ComfyUI was not told about the change, so reloading the page right after **Put in** (or Ctrl+Z) brought back the old, missing name and its red mark. Each put-in is now one step of ComfyUI's history, saved like any edit of your own.
- **Model Check finds renamed models by fingerprint again (按指纹找回改名的模型)**: when a workflow's model was renamed or moved and no scan had hashed the file yet, Model Check failed to hash the same-size files itself (an error in the background) and called the model missing. It now hashes them and finds it.
- **Model Check is honest about same-name files (模型检查不再把同名当成同一个文件)**: for a workflow without fingerprints, a missing LoRA or checkpoint with a file of the same name on this computer was listed as "the same file under another name or folder" and counted among those to put in at once. It is now a candidate you take or leave on its row: "same name", or "same name and exactly the same size, very likely the one" (listed first). VAE, CLIP and other foundation components still go by fingerprint only. Model Check now also checks GGUF models and finds upscale models moved to another folder. Each one carries a level next to its name: ●●● sure (same fingerprint), ●●○ likely, ●○○ maybe, ○○○ unclear / does not match / not found.

### 🔍 Scanning and model information (扫描与模型信息)
- **Scan page (扫描页)**: the rail's scan button and Home's "Scan model folders" open a page instead of the old seven-switch dialog. It shows how many models are matched on Civitai, not matched, and not scanned yet; one button scans the new ones, with progress in the page (it runs in the background, so the window can be closed). Offline scanning, renaming files on disk, fetching everything again, picking models and the Civitai API key are in **Scan settings**; the two risky switches turn themselves off after each scan. The "record model fingerprints in workflows" switch moved to Settings → Model settings.
- **The scan page in plain words (扫描页说人话)**: it says what a scan does (the file's fingerprint is looked up on Civitai), and why a model was not found: not published on Civitai (voice, upscale and detection models mostly come from HuggingFace or GitHub), trained or merged by you, or changed after download; it works as usual, and you can add its download link. A model Civitai does not know that is no known image model says it may be a voice or upscale model. Model files in other formats (.pth, .ckpt, .bin…), which scans never read, are listed under **Other formats** with why, so they are no longer a mystery.
- **A tidier scan page (扫描页更清爽)**: the scan page now holds only the counts, the scan button, the last scan in one card and a card saying how the next scan goes. **Not found**, **Not scanned yet**, the other formats and the last scan's models each open their own page with **‹ Back to scan**, a line on why those models are there, one button for all of them (scan these / look them up again) and the list; a model opened from it comes back to the same list where you were. **Show the result** after a scan opens the last scan's page. The old fold-out **Advanced** is now **Scan settings**, a page of its own like the lists: its card on the scan page shows how the next scan goes (all models or the picked ones, online or offline, renaming files…, with the ones that change files in yellow), and the page asks one question per card, which models as two choices side by side and each option with a switch, the ones that change files or take long marked so, the online-only ones under their own heading (faded for an offline scan).
- **Unmatched models can be looked up again (没匹配上的模型可以重新查找)**: a model that Civitai did not know, or that was scanned while Civitai could not be reached, used to stay "local only" for good unless every model was fetched again (which also replaced your own covers). **Look up the N unmatched models again** retries just those, keeps your covers, and reuses the saved hash when the file is unchanged, so it takes seconds instead of re-hashing gigabytes.
- **Which models, and what a scan did (是哪些模型、扫描做了什么)**: on the scan page, **Not matched** and **Not scanned yet** open a list of those models, with their type and folder; unmatched ones say why (Civitai does not have the file, Civitai could not be reached then, or it was an offline scan) and the base model inferred. Each row opens the model (Back returns to the scan page), scans it or looks it up again, or searches Civitai for its name. After a scan the page shows its result: when, how long, how many matched, inferred, failed, covers downloaded and files renamed, and each model it changed with what happened; the progress box says the same and, when it floats over the canvas, has **Show the result**. A model's card scan looks an unmatched model up again and its message says what it found.
- **Online and offline in one scan (联网和离线在同一次扫描里)**: a scan asks Civitai first and infers the base model from the file for what Civitai does not know, as before; now it also remembers why. When Civitai stops answering (two models in a row), the rest of the scan infers from the files right away instead of waiting out every timeout, and the next online scan looks those models up again by itself, as it does for models from an offline scan. Models Civitai answered "not found" for wait for **Look up again**. A model that fails (a locked file, say) no longer ends the scan of the others; it is listed as failed with the reason.
- **Your edits come first (你改的永远优先)**: names, notes and download links you set in the model editor now have a file of their own next to the model (`<model>.anomalous.json`) that scans never write, and they win over Civitai's information, which wins over what is inferred from the file. Edits saved by earlier versions keep working. Editing a model that was never scanned used to make it look scanned, so scans skipped it for good; it is now scanned like any new model.
- **Covers you chose are never replaced (你换的封面不会被替换)**: a cover counts as Civitai's only while it is the very image the scan downloaded; any other cover is yours. **Fetch scanned models again** now keeps your covers (and names and notes), updates only Civitai's own, and asks before it starts.
- **Where a model's information comes from (信息来源标注)**: cards look as before for models matched on Civitai; a base model inferred from the file shows as "≈ SDXL" with a dashed outline, and a model not scanned yet says so, each with a tooltip on what to do. The detail header adds the source (Civitai, inferred from the file, not scanned) and which fields you edited.
- **Faster rescans (重复扫描更快)**: a scan no longer downloads every cover and Civitai model page again for models whose information is already on disk; only new or missing ones go to the network.
- **Model types at the top (模型页顶部类型标签)**: the models page starts with a chip per model type (Checkpoint, LoRA, VAE…) and how many models it holds; a chip shows that whole type, subfolders included, so the grid no longer looks like it holds one folder's models. Picking a folder in the list narrows the grid to that folder, shown as a chip with ✕ that goes back to the whole type. The browser remembers the type you picked last.
- **Model sources on the Models page (模型来源进了模型页)**: the Sources chip at the end of the model types now shows, in place of the cards, where each model is downloaded, for **This workflow** or **All models**, filtered by with / without a link. Every link can be typed or pasted straight into its row (any site, not only Civitai), found automatically, opened and saved with one button; a link is kept in the model's own information (the same as the editor's download link) and, for this workflow's models, in the workflow too, so it travels when the workflow is shared. The list can go on the canvas as a note or to the clipboard. Model Check's **Where to download** opens it on This workflow. The separate pop-up, its lock-then-edit links and its four ways of saving are gone.
- **GGUF and .pth models show up (GGUF、.pth 模型能看到了)**: the Models page, the folder list's counts, search and model lookups only knew `.safetensors`, `.ckpt` and `.pt`, in lower case, so quantized GGUF models (Wan, Flux, Qwen…), most upscalers (`.pth`), `.sft` and `.bin` files and names like `MODEL.SAFETENSORS` were missing. They are all listed now. Scans still read only `.safetensors`; the others stay under **Not scanned yet**.

### 🗑️ Safer with your files (删除更安全)
- **Deleting goes to the Recycle Bin (删除进回收站)**: deleting a model (with its info and covers), an output image, generated audio, a recipe (with its versions and images), a material, a prompt note or a parameter note, replacing or resetting a cover, and the duplicate copy a file rename finds, all move the files to the system Recycle Bin instead of erasing them. On a USB stick or network drive, which has no Recycle Bin, nothing is deleted and the message says to delete it by hand. The confirmations say where things go. An unused route that could erase every `.civitai.info` in the library (with the names you set) is gone.

### 🪶 Lighter on memory (更省内存)
- **Model cards show up sooner (模型卡片出得更快)**: card thumbnails used to live in ComfyUI's temp folder, which ComfyUI empties on every start, so the first visit after each restart remade them all. They are now kept in ComfyUI's user folder (still at most 256 MB) and survive restarts. Opening a model type also makes the missing ones in the background, one at a time, so scrolling down finds them ready; the first screen of cards loads before the rest.
- **Video covers show a still first (视频封面先显示第一帧)**: a video cover now shows its first frame right away and downloads nothing until it plays. Playing on hover is the default now (it was always-play, though the setting called hover "recommended"), so fewer videos decode at once; "Always play" is still in Settings.
- The output gallery shows 512 px thumbnails instead of full images (cached like model cards), so scrolling through hundreds of outputs no longer fills the browser's memory and graphics memory with full-size pictures. Dragging an image to the canvas and the large viewer still use the original. When the browser has been closed for 90 seconds, its gallery images are released too, like the model grid.
