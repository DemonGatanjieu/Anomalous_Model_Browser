If you publicly distribute a modified version or fork:

- use a distinct primary product name and a distinct logo so users can readily distinguish it from the official project;
- identify it clearly as an unofficial fork or modified version;
- you may use wording such as "Based on Anomalous Model Browser" to describe its origin;
- preserve the copyright and permission notices required by the MIT License.

如果您公开分发修改版本或分支：

- 应使用不同的主要产品名称和不同的 Logo，使用户能够清楚地区分其与官方项目；

These branding guidelines do not require renaming private modifications that are not distributed to others.
